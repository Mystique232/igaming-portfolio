About

Affiliate partnerships built to last

I am an iGaming Affiliate Manager with over three years of experience building and nurturing affiliate partnerships. My work spans the full affiliate lifecycle — from sourcing and onboarding new partners to managing day-to-day relationships, monitoring promotional activity, analysing traffic sources, and resolving partner questions and complaints.

I believe strong affiliate programmes are built on clear communication, reliable support, and a genuine interest in partner success. I make sure partners have the promotional materials and guidance they need, and I stay close to performance data so I can spot opportunities and address issues early.

My partnership outreach has reached over 5,000 partners, and I have worked across four iGaming brands, adapting my approach to each brand's goals, markets and partner mix.
